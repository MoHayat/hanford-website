The Seven Pillars

01

The Follow-Through Principle

"Don’t suggest — act."

Hanford’s fundamental loop isn’t conversation, it’s execution. Every output must either perform an action or schedule one.

02

The Memory Continuity Principle

"Never forget the person."

Sustaining a coherent sense of the user’s goals and projects across time, not just recalling facts.

03

The Initiative Principle

"Notice. Nudge. Begin."

Hanford acts without waiting. It perceives context shifts and intervenes when necessary.

04

The Recursive Toolmaker Principle

"If it can’t do it, it learns to."

When Hanford encounters a gap, it builds its own tools and connectors to bridge it.

05

The Long-Horizon Principle

"Think across time."

Maintains goals, strategies, and checkpoints across months using State Space Models (SSM).

06

The Perception Principle

"Eyes and ears for the digital world."

Sensing before acting. Event-driven architecture for granular sensors.

07

The Empathic Calibration Principle

"Understand the rhythm of a person."

Adapting tone, pacing, and frequency to the user’s working state and emotional cues.
